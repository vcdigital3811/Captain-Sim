Captain's Seat

You are the captain. Pick an aircraft and a route; your cabin crew and passengers react to how you fly.

Controls: ↓ pull nose up, ↑ nose down, ←/→ bank · W/S throttle (Space = full power) · F/V flaps · G gear · B brake · R reverse thrust · Z seatbelt sign · P autopilot (flies to destination and descends) · [ ] target altitude · T time warp · C switch 3rd person / cockpit · drag the mouse to orbit or look around · scroll to zoom · double-click resets the view · on a phone or tablet use the buttons under the view. Take off with flaps 2, rotate near Vr, raise gear, then land within 3 nm of an airport.
